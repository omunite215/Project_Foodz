# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately through GitHub:
**[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/omunite215/Project_Foodz/security/advisories/new)**.
Do not open a public issue for anything that could put users at risk.

A useful report includes what you found, the steps to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do
with it. You will get an acknowledgement within three working days and a note on the fix once it
is planned.

## Scope

- This repository and the demo deployment linked from the README.
- Out of scope: denial-of-service and volume testing, social engineering, physical attacks, and
  issues in third-party services (payment, realtime, email and hosting providers) that should be
  reported to them.

The public demo accounts are shared and reset regularly; please do not try to lock other people
out of them.

## Supported versions

Only the `main` branch is maintained.
